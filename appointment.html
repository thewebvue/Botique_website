Before you book

How long does a custom outfit take?

Blouses take 2 to 3 weeks. Kurta sets 2 to 3 weeks. Sarees with stitched blouses 3 to 5 weeks. Bridal lehengas and sherwanis 6 to 8 weeks. We confirm a date before you pay.

Can I bring my own saree or fabric?

Yes. We stitch blouses, lehengas and kurtas from your fabric, and we will tell you if it needs lining or a different cut.

What does a custom outfit cost?

Prices on the collection pages are starting prices. Final quotes depend on fabric, embroidery and lining. You get a written quote after the design step.

Do you offer alterations?

Free for 30 days on anything we made. After that we charge a small fee. We also alter outfits made elsewhere.
