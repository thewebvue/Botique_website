04 The studio

A small studio, long memory

Pattern makers, tailors and embroiderers working in one room, with every customer's measure card on the shelf.

(Story) Our story

It started with a notebook

Ezhil means grace. The studio began with one machine and a notebook in which our founder wrote down her cousins' measurements before every festival.

Today that notebook is a shelf of measure cards, and the team is larger, but we still work the same way: sketch, swatch, cut, fit, fit again.

“A good fit is a conversation, and it is never finished in one visit.”

(Habits) What we believe

Three habits we keep

  1. i

    Fit before flourish

    Embroidery is added only when the cut is right. A plain blouse that fits beats a heavy one that does not.

  2. ii

    Honest about cloth

    We tell you if a fabric will not suit the weather, the season or the cut, even if it costs us the sale.

  3. iii

    Made to be re-worn

    Generous seam allowance, let-down hems and free alterations for 30 days, so the outfit grows with you.

(Cloth) The cloth room

We source and stitch

Silks come directly from weavers in Kanchipuram and Varanasi, cottons from Madurai and Jaipur. Spools and trims are kept in the same room, so you can hold the thread against the fabric before it is cut.

Bringing your own saree? We will check it for weight, lining and stretch before we promise a design.
