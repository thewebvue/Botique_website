03 Bespoke

Sketch to second fitting

A custom outfit takes weeks, not days. This is what happens in them, and what we need from you.

  1. i

    Consult

    Bring a picture, a saree or just an occasion. We talk about the event, your budget and what you will actually wear again.

  2. ii

    Measure

    We take 27 measurements in inches and note your posture. They go onto a card that stays with us for every future order.

  3. iii

    Design

    A sketch with fabric swatches and a written quote. You approve it before anything is cut.

  4. iv

    Stitch

    Cutting, stitching and hand embroidery under one roof, with a progress photo at each stage.

  5. v

    Fit & finish

    Two fittings, then final pressing. Free alterations for 30 days after delivery.

(Card) Measure card

Twenty-seven numbers that stay with us

At your first fitting we take 27 measurements in inches, plus notes on posture, shoulder slope and how you like to wear a blouse. They go on a card that we keep, so a repeat order never starts from scratch.

Weight changes before a wedding? Tell us. We cut with seam allowance in the right places and re-check at both fittings.

(Blouse) What we measure

The blouse card

All in inches. You do not need to measure at home, but this is what we will ask.

  • BustAround the fullest part, tape level at the back.
  • Under-bustJust below the bust, where a band would sit.
  • WaistAt the narrowest point. Do not pull the tape tight.
  • HipAround the fullest part of the hip, about 8 inches below the waist.
  • ShoulderFrom the neck base to the shoulder edge.
  • Across backBetween the two shoulder edges at the back.
  • Sleeve lengthFrom the shoulder edge to the wrist or where you want it.
  • Blouse lengthFrom the shoulder to where the blouse should end.

(FAQ) Questions

Before you book

How long does a custom outfit take?

Blouses take 2 to 3 weeks. Kurta sets 2 to 3 weeks. Sarees with stitched blouses 3 to 5 weeks. Bridal lehengas and sherwanis 6 to 8 weeks. We confirm a date before you pay.

Can I bring my own saree or fabric?

Yes. We stitch blouses, lehengas and kurtas from your fabric, and we will tell you if it needs lining or a different cut.

What does a custom outfit cost?

Prices on the collection pages are starting prices. Final quotes depend on fabric, embroidery and lining. You get a written quote after the design step.

Do you offer alterations?

Free for 30 days on anything we made. After that we charge a small fee. We also alter outfits made elsewhere.

Can you rush an order?

Sometimes. Blouses can be done in 5 days. Bridal pieces cannot be rushed safely, so please come early.

Do I need to book a fitting?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome to browse, but consultations and measurements are by appointment so you have a designer's full attention.
