(04) How we work

Five steps, no shortcuts

The full process
  1. i

    Consult

    Bring a picture, a saree or just an occasion. We talk about the event, your budget and what you will actually wear again.

  2. ii

    Measure

    We take 27 measurements in inches and note your posture. They go onto a card that stays with us for every future order.

  3. iii

    Design

    A sketch with fabric swatches and a written quote. You approve it before anything is cut.

  4. iv

    Stitch

    Cutting, stitching and hand embroidery under one roof, with a progress photo at each stage.

  5. v

    Fit & finish

    Two fittings, then final pressing. Free alterations for 30 days after delivery.

(06) Fabrics

Start with the cloth

  • Kanchipuram silk

    Heavy, woven with real zari. Bridal and temple wear.

  • Banarasi silk

    Brocade with raised patterns. Dupattas and sherwanis.

  • Chanderi

    Sheer and airy silk cotton. Summer weddings.

  • Mulmul cotton

    Very fine cotton. Daily sarees and kids' wear.

  • Tussar silk

    Dry and textured. Holds embroidery well.

  • Raw silk

    Structured with a slub. Lehengas and sherwanis.

  • Georgette

    Fluid and light. Festive lehengas and gowns.

  • Velvet

    Rich and warm. Winter blouses and jackets.
